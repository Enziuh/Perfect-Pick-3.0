How prizes work

Perfect Pick Rules

The active rule set for each prize period is shown on the Prizes tab.

New Rules

3.0

Weekly entry: $5 per active player.

Perfect Pick: Pick every game correctly for the week.

If one player gets a Perfect Pick: That player wins the full weekly pot.

If multiple players get a Perfect Pick: They split the weekly pot evenly.

If nobody gets a Perfect Pick: The full weekly pot rolls into that prize period's Monthly Pot.

At the end of the prize period: 70% of the remaining Monthly Pot becomes the monthly standings prize pool, and 30% moves into the End-of-Season Pot.

Monthly standings payout: 1st receives 60%, 2nd receives 25%, and 3rd receives 15% of the monthly standings prize pool.

Season payout: 1st receives 60%, 2nd receives 25%, and 3rd receives 15% of the End-of-Season Pot.

Ties: Monthly and season ties use the same occupied-position rule. The percentages for the tied positions are combined and split evenly among the tied players. Weekly Perfect Pick ties split the weekly pot evenly.

Old Rules

LEGACY

Weekly entry: $5 per active player.

Perfect Pick: A player had to correctly pick every game that week to win the weekly pot.

If one player got a Perfect Pick: That player won the full weekly pot.

If multiple players got a Perfect Pick: They split the weekly pot evenly.

If nobody got a Perfect Pick: The entire weekly pot went directly into the End-of-Season Pot.

No monthly payout: Under the Old Rules there was no monthly standings prize or 70/30 monthly split.

Player help

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for using the Perfect Pick dashboard and understanding the prize tracker.

Where can I see my picks?

Open the Picks tab. It defaults to the current week, and you can use the week dropdown to review earlier weeks.

How do I see how everyone picked?

The Picks tab shows every player's submitted picks plus the percentage of players who selected each team.

When do correct and incorrect picks update?

They update as game results in the Results sheet are marked Final. Winning/correct teams receive a subtle team-color highlight.

What is a Perfect Pick?

A Perfect Pick means a player correctly selected the winner of every game included in that week's form.

Why does the Monthly Pot change?

Under the New Rules, a finalized weekly pot rolls into the Monthly Pot when nobody gets a Perfect Pick that week.

How is the monthly standings prize paid?

At the end of a prize period, 70% of the Monthly Pot becomes the monthly standings prize pool. That pool is paid 60% to 1st place, 25% to 2nd place, and 15% to 3rd place. Ties use the same occupied-position split used for the season standings.

What does “Projected” prize money mean?

Projected amounts show what the prize would be if the standings or prize period ended right now. They can change as more games and weeks are completed.

Where can I see weekly, monthly, and season standings?

Use the Weekly, Monthly, and Season tabs. Weekly and Monthly include selectors so you can review earlier weeks and prize periods.

Where can I see which rule set is being used?

Open the Prizes tab. Each prize period shows both Old and New rule options, with the active rule set highlighted.

What is the difference between the Old and New Rules?

Both rule sets pay the weekly pot for a Perfect Pick. Under the Old Rules, a week with no Perfect Pick sends the entire weekly pot directly to the End-of-Season Pot. Under the New Rules, that weekly pot rolls into the Monthly Pot; 70% of the monthly pot becomes the monthly standings prize pool and 30% goes to the End-of-Season Pot.

What happens if players are tied?

Ties are handled consistently across Perfect Pick. Weekly Perfect Pick winners split that week's pot evenly. For Monthly and Season standings, tied players combine the payout percentages for the positions they occupy and split that combined amount evenly. For example, a two-way tie for 1st combines 1st and 2nd place (60% + 25% = 85%), so each tied player receives 42.5%, and the next player receives the 15% third-place share.

Why might the weekly pot change?

The weekly pot is based on the number of active players for that week multiplied by the $5 entry amount.
